FAQs
What's the difference between JM and SUBD supervisory organizations?

When you search for a supervisory organization, it may appear twice. SUBD holds staffing information for classified and banded positions. JM holds staffing information for temps, casuals, students, and sometimes academic employees. In most cases, you'll want the SUBD organization.

How do I check which manager role I have?

Your role determines which reports you can run. Open the Workers I Support report and look under All Supervisory Roles in the Roles field.

How can I find out who supports my employee in HR?

On the employee's profile, open the Job tab, then Support Roles. It lists the employee's HR partner, who can help with questions like whether the employee was paid.

How do I save a report I use often?
  1. Type Manage Favorites in the Workday search box.
  2. Find the report under Favorite Tasks/Reports or Favorite Custom Reports.
  3. Select OK. The report now appears in your Favorites.

Probationary periods New

Non-exempt new hires and transfers have a 90-day probationary period. Workday sends a review task to your Inbox 30 days before it ends, so you have time to assess the employee's performance and record the outcome.

1. Complete the questionnaire

Open the Probation Questionnaire for Manager task from your Inbox. Answer each question based on the employee's performance during probation, and add comments where helpful.

2. Select the outcome

In the Submit Probation Period Review task, choose Successfully Completed, Extend Probation, or Terminate, then select Submit.

3. Review the document with your employee

Meet with the employee to go over their Probation Summary Review Document. They'll confirm in Workday that they received it.

Where to find it
Inbox → Probation Questionnaire for Manager
Workday search box → Review Probation Period by Supervisory Organizations report

FAQs
What happens after I select an outcome?

Successfully Completed closes the process, and the employee receives their probation documentation, including a copy of the questionnaire, in their Workday record.

Extend Probation sends the request to your HR rep for review. If approved, a new probation period begins.

Terminate sends the request to your HR office, which will work with you to start the termination process.

What happens if I extend an employee's probation?

HR reviews your questionnaire responses, comments, and reason for the extension. The employee receives a Probation Summary Review document saying their probation is being extended. If HR approves, new end and review dates are set. On the new review date, a Review Probation Period task arrives in your Inbox, and you complete the questionnaire and outcome steps again.

What happens if I select Terminate?

HR reviews your questionnaire responses and comments, then works with you on next steps. These may include meeting with the employee and reviewing termination documentation.

Where can my employee find their probation documents?

On their own Workday profile, under the Personal menu option, Documents tab.

How can I track where my team's probation reviews stand?

Run the Review Probation Period by Supervisory Organizations report. It lists workers whose probationary period falls within the dates you choose, along with each review's outcome and any steps still waiting for action.

What if I missed a question or the deadline?

Contact your HR rep right away for guidance.

Does this process apply to union employees?

For union employees, check the applicable collective bargaining agreement for criteria and procedures on probationary extensions and terminations.

FAQs: reviews
How do performance reviews work?

When your team member submits their Self-Evaluation, you'll get a Manager Evaluation item in your Inbox. You can also open My Team's Performance Reviews to see where each direct report is in the process. If the current step says Complete Manager Evaluation, select that employee's review to begin.

Can I complete my evaluation before the employee finishes their self-evaluation?

Employees start the process by answering the Self-Evaluation questions, and only they can see their responses while it's in progress. Share deadlines with your team so you have enough time to complete Evaluations for your direct reports.

How can I send a review back to an employee?

If an employee needs to revise after selecting Submit, you can send it back. In the My Team Management app, go to Manager Reports by Category → Performance → My Team's Performance Reviews. Find the review, open its actions menu, and select Business Process → Manual Send Back.

How can my manager view my direct reports' reviews?

Your manager isn't part of the formal review steps, but can run the Employee Review by Supervisory Organization report:

  1. Type the report name in the Workday search box.
  2. Select the organization by typing in the manager's name.
  3. To see reviews for the whole organization, check Include Subordinate Organizations.
FAQs: goals
What's the difference between the Personal Goals, Goal Details, and Organizational Goals tabs?

Personal Goals shows goals you create for yourself and goals your leader creates for you. Goal Details shows the same goals in a table view. Organizational Goals shows goals set at the college or unit level.

What's the difference between deleting a goal and marking it "On Hold"?

Remove permanently deletes the goal from Workday, and it can't be recovered. Changing the status to On Hold moves the goal to the bottom of the Personal Goals tab, where you can update it later.
