Workday for People Leaders

Anyone who has employees reporting to them will have the Workday role "Manager," regardless of your specific title at Cornell.

With Workday's manager self-service, people leaders are empowered with necessary and relevant job, position, compensation, labor distribution (called costing data in Workday), performance reviews, goals, recruiting and time and attendance information. This includes access to numerous Workday reports with information about your team to support all HR-related decisions. In addition, you will be required to approve Time Entry (timesheets for hourly employees only) and Time Off Requests, which will be routed to you in your inbox.

Inbox

Your Inbox will show notifications of tasks related to you or your employees and items that have been routed to you for approval, such as Hourly Time Entry and Time Off requests.

Manager Insights Hub

The Manager Insights Hub consolidates various tasks, reports, and insights about your team into one central location, streamlining the ability of people leaders to support their teams. People leaders have quick access to important tasks, reports, and personalized team member recommendations, such as connections, mentors, gigs, and learning opportunities. The Manager Insights Hub is available in your Global Navigation Menu.

Workday for Managers Guide

The Workday for Managers Guide walks people leaders through using Workday as a decision-making tool. It's a good place to start if you're new to managing in Workday, or if you want a refresher on where to find information about your team.

Navigating Workday

Learn the main parts of your Workday Home page, how to search for people, tasks, and reports, and how to add apps to your Menu or move the ones you use most to the top of the list.

Finding Employee and Team Information

See what's available on each tab of an employee's profile, including Job, Compensation, Time Off, Contact, and Performance. The guide also explains how to use the Related Actions button to view details and take action on an employee.

Supervisory Organizations

Look up any supervisory organization to see its members, positions that are open or unavailable to fill, and the HR and Compensation partners who support it.

Reports

Find out which reports are available to you based on your manager role. Reports are grouped into five categories: Workforce, Compensation, Time Off, Recruiting, and Performance Dialogue. The guide also shows how to add reports you use often to your Favorites.

What is the difference between JM and SUBD supervisory organizations?
When you search for a supervisory organization, it may appear with two designations. SUBD holds staffing information for classified and banded positions. JM holds staffing information for temps, casuals, students, and sometimes academic employees. In most cases, you will want to select the SUBD organization.
How can I check which manager role I have in Workday?
The reports you can access depend on your manager role. To check your role, open the Workers I Support report and look under All Supervisory Roles in the Roles field.
How can I find out who supports my employee in HR?
From the employee's profile, click on the Job tab and then Support Roles. This shows the employee's HR partner, who can help with questions such as whether the employee was paid.
How do I add a report to my Favorites?
  1. Type Manage Favorites in the Workday search box.
  2. Search for the report under Favorite Tasks/Reports or Favorite Custom Reports.
  3. Click OK.

Time Management

Approving Time Worked

Managers can use the My Team Management dashboard to approve submitted timeblocks for multiple employees (including students) within the two-week pay period. The Workday Inbox can be used by managers for submitted time of an employee within a specific week. Note: We recommend reviewing and approving time on a weekly basis. To learn about the specific steps to approve time, please see the Approving Time job aid.

Viewing Time Off

Workday is the system to record and track time off balances for both hourly and salaried employees. To view your employees' time off balance, click on the Team Time Off worklet. There are various options, including a Time Off Leave and Calendar view. To see current balances you may wish to select the Time Off Summary view from that page.

Approving Time Off

Time Off Requests will appear in your Inbox for review and approval. To learn about the specific steps to approve time, please see the Approving Time Off job aid.

Delegating Approval of Time Off

Delegation is used to allow another employee to review and approve on your behalf. As a manager, you may wish to delegate your Time Entry and Time Off approval responsibilities to another. This can be done for short periods of time or on an on-going basis. In addition, an entire inbox can be delegated to another individual during a leave or vacation. Please see the Delegate Quick Reference guide to walk you through the necessary steps.

Employee Information

Click on the My Team icon on your Home page. The My Team page is composed of windows that provide different views into your direct reports. Click on the link with their name and photo of each employee to view more details about that individual.

Job changes

To examine a specific employee's history, click on the Timeline option with the My Team icon on the homepage. This view contains a historical record of all the changes made to a worker, including changes to their job, position, compensation, etc. It is arranged by date.

Compensation

To examine a specific employee's compensation, click on the hyperlink with employee's name (either from the My Team worklet or wherever their name is visible) and then click on their Compensation tab. Any questions about compensation should be directed to your local Human Resource Office.

Performance Management

How do Performance Reviews work?
When your team member has submitted their Self-Evaluation, you will receive a Manager Evaluation item in your Workday Inbox. You may also click on the Team Performance icon on your home page and click under View, My Team's Performance Reviews. This report will show you the current status of each of your direct reports. If the current step states Complete Manager Evaluation, you may click on that employee's performance review link to begin your evaluation.
Can I complete my Evaluation in advance of the employee completing their Self-Evaluation?
Employees begin the Performance Review process by answering the questions in the Self-Evaluation portion of Workday. Only you can view your responses while the Self- Evaluation is in progress. As such, it will be important to communicate deadlines so you have sufficient time to complete Evaluations for your direct reports.
How can I SEND BACK a review to an employee?
If an employee needs to make further revisions after they clicked on Submit button, you can "Send Back" their review, as needed. Within the Team Management app, in the Manager Reports by Category section, select Performance and then My Team's Performance Reviews. Locate the correct review in the Performance Review column and click on the and then select Business Process > Manual Send Back.
How can my manager view my direct reports' reviews?
Your manager is still able to view your direct reports' reviews, however, it is not a step in the formal performance review process. Instead, they may generate a report Employee Review by Supervisory Organization.
  1. Type the name of the report in the search box.
  2. Select the organization by typing in the manager's name.
  3. To see all the employees' reviews for the whole organization, click on the Include Subordinate Organizations box.
What is the difference between the Personal Goals, Goal Details, and Organizational Goals tabs?
The Personal Goals tab shows goals that you create for yourself, and that your leader creates for you. The Goal Details tab contains all of the basic goal information from the Personal Goals tab in a table-style view. The Organization Goals tab is where goals that are set at the College or Unit level would be viewed.
What is the difference between deleting a goal and marking its status as "On Hold"?
When handling a goal that may not be used during a given year there are two options, one is to Remove the goal. Doing this would permanently delete the goal from Workday (with no way to recover this information). Another option is to change that goal's Status to On Hold. This moves the goal to the bottom of your Personal Goals profile tab where it can be updated in the future.

Probationary Periods

A probationary period gives managers a defined time to review a new team member's performance and record the outcome in Workday. For non-exempt new hires and transfers, the probationary period is 90 days. Workday will automatically send a task to your Inbox 30 days before the probationary period ends. For more details, please see the Probationary Period Policy.

Completing the Questionnaire

Open the Probation Questionnaire for Manager task from your Workday Inbox. Answer each question based on the employee's performance during their probationary period, and add comments as appropriate.

Selecting the Outcome

Complete the Submit Probation Period Review task by selecting one of three outcomes. Successfully Completed closes the process, and the employee receives their probation documentation in their Workday record. Extend Probation sends the request to your HR representative for review, and a new probation period is set if approved. Terminate sends the request to your HR office, which will work with you on termination processing.

Reviewing the Documentation with Your Employee

Once the probationary period is successfully completed, the employee receives a Probation Summary Review Document that summarizes the review, including the questionnaire results. Meet with the employee to go over the document. After reviewing it, the employee will confirm in Workday that they received it.

What happens if I extend an employee's probationary period?
HR reviews your questionnaire responses, comments, and reason for the extension. The employee will receive a Probation Summary Review document indicating their probation is being extended. If the extension is approved, new probation end and review dates are set. When the new review date arrives, a Review Probation Period task will be sent to your Inbox, and you will complete the questionnaire and outcome steps again.
What happens if I select Terminate as the outcome?
HR reviews your questionnaire responses and comments, then partners with you on next steps. These may include meeting with the employee and reviewing termination documentation as part of the termination process.
Where can my employee find their probation documentation?
The employee can view the document on their own Workday worker profile, under the Personal menu option, on the Documents tab.
Is there a report I can use to monitor the process?
Yes. The Review Probation Period by Supervisory Organizations report lists workers whose probationary period falls within the dates you select. It shows the overall review outcome and any steps currently awaiting action.
What if I accidentally skip a question or miss the deadline?
Contact your HR Rep immediately for further guidance.
Does this process apply to union employees?
For union employees, please review the applicable collective bargaining agreement for criteria and procedures for probationary extensions or terminations.
